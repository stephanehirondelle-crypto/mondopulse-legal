Privacy Policy

Effective: September 18, 2026 · Mondopulse mobile app (iOS and Android)

In short

  • Mondopulse works without an account and without any server of ours. We do not know who you are.
  • Your settings (chosen country, compared countries, free discoveries used) stay on your phone. Uninstalling erases them.
  • Figures are bundled inside the app. To show a figure’s history since 1960, the app queries the public World Bank API directly: it then sees your IP address, like any website. Nothing else is sent.
  • No ads, no trackers, no usage analytics, no cookies.
  • If you subscribe to Mondopulse Plus, the purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play and verified for us by RevenueCat under an anonymous identifier.

1. Controller

Mondopulse is published by Stéphane Hirondelle, independent publisher (“we”). Contact: contact@nutriflow-app.net. Postal address available on request.

2. What we do not collect

  • No name, e-mail, phone number or account.
  • No advertising identifier, geolocation or device fingerprint.
  • No crash reporting, ad network, social module or audience measurement.
  • No sale or sharing of data.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores locally: your “my country” choice, the compare list, the number of free discoveries used, your subscription status (yes/no) and a cache of charts already viewed (7 days). This never leaves the device and disappears on uninstall.

4. Data sent to third parties

World Bank (api.worldbank.org) — when you open a figure, the app requests that figure’s history for the displayed country. The request contains the country code and indicator code; the server sees your IP address. Policy: worldbank.org.

Apple / Google — installation and purchases go through your App Store or Google Play account under their own policies.

RevenueCat — for Mondopulse Plus, the purchase receipt is verified by RevenueCat, our subscription provider, under a random identifier generated on the device (never your name or e-mail). Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Google Fonts — the app’s fonts may be loaded from fonts.googleapis.com (IP address visible to Google).

5. Your rights

We hold no data about you: there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. For local data, uninstall the app. For purchases, contact Apple or Google. You may still write to us with any question, or lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

6. Children

Mondopulse is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, whatever their age.

7. Changes

Any change will be published on this page with its effective date.
